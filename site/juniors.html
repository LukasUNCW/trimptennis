Intro to tennis

Grom's

Ages 6–12. First racquets, first rallies, and the habits that make the rest of the game easy.

Players learn with orange and green dot balls — they travel slower and bounce lower, so a beginner can actually rally instead of chasing. Racquets and balls are sized to each child's age and ability, so nobody is swinging equipment built for an adult.

Come Tuesday, Thursday, or both — attendance is flexible, and classes are capped at 18 players so everybody gets time on court with a coach.

Intermediate program

Shredder's

Ages 9–16. Live ball, point play, and match play four afternoons a week.

Shredder's runs a play-based format — less standing in line, more live ball hitting, points, and actual matches. Players are grouped by age and skill level, so a strong ten-year-old isn't stuck rallying with someone twice their size, and nobody is out of their depth.

Before joining: players need to be able to rally, keep score, and play official games. If that's not there yet, Grom's is the right starting point — and the coaches will tell you honestly which fits.
