Beginner to advanced

Adult programs

John and Logan coach adults across Wilmington — clinics, team practices, and private lessons, from a first-ever lesson through advanced match play. No prior experience assumed, and no age at which it's too late to start.

What's on offer

Three ways to play

Ladies

Team clinics & lessons

Clinics and team lessons for women at every level, run at the UNCW courts and out around the area. Cardio tennis is part of the mix if you want the workout as much as the match.

  • UNCW courts — the main home
  • Also Topsail, Hampstead & Carolina Beach
  • Cardio tennis available
Men's

Team practices & lessons

Fast paced, with the focus on tactics for advanced play. All live ball hitting and point play — you'll spend the session moving and hitting rather than standing in a queue.

  • Tactical work for advanced play
  • Live ball and point play throughout
  • Cardio tennis available
One to one

Private lessons

Private, semi-private, and small group lessons, built around whatever you're trying to fix. Open to every age and every standard — beginners included.

  • Private & semi-private
  • Small group options
  • All ages and skill levels
Joining

How to get started

Adult sessions are arranged directly with the coaches rather than booked off a fixed timetable — the group you join depends on your standard, which day suits you, and which court you'd rather play on.

Rates aren't listed here because they depend on the format and the group size. Email John or Logan, or send a message through the contact form, and they'll tell you what fits and what it costs.

If you'd rather we call you, use the contact form and choose phone — it goes straight to the office.
