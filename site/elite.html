Seahawk Elite

Year-round training for ranked, high school, and college-bound players. Monday to Thursday, 5:00 – 7:00 PM, directed by head pro Logan Trimp.

The program

Two hours a day, all year

Elite runs Monday through Thursday, 5:00 – 7:00 PM, year round, and enrollment is month to month rather than by term — players train through the tournament calendar instead of stopping and restarting.

Auto-draft billing is paused during high school season, so families aren't paying for court time while their player is competing for their school. Multi-child discounts are available.

Who it's for

Elite is a competitive squad, not an open clinic. New players qualify by meeting at least one of these:

  • A sectional or national ranking
  • Currently on a high school team
  • State-ranked, or an intermediate-to-advanced junior competitor

Approval required. Every new player is assessed by head pro Logan Trimp before joining, so the group stays level. Start by enrolling below or emailing Logan directly — he'll arrange a look.
